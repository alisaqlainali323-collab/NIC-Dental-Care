Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a dental checkup cost in Rawalpindi?
A complete dental checkup including examination and cleaning starts from just PKR 2,500. We also offer new patient specials and family discounts. All prices are transparent with no hidden charges.
Does root canal treatment hurt?
No! With modern anesthesia and techniques, root canal treatment is virtually painless. Most patients report that the procedure feels similar to getting a filling.
Do you accept health insurance?
Yes, we accept all major health insurance plans. We also file claims on your behalf. For patients without insurance, we offer affordable payment plans.
At what age should I bring my child for first dental visit?
The Pakistan Dental Association recommends bringing your child for their first dental visit by age 1 or within 6 months of their first tooth appearing.

Teeth Whitening

Professional teeth whitening is a safe and effective way to brighten your smile. Our in-office treatment uses advanced Zoom technology to remove years of stains from coffee, tea, wine, and aging. The procedure takes about 60 minutes and can lighten your teeth by up to 8 shades. Most patients experience minimal sensitivity that resolves within 24 hours. Results can last up to 3 years with proper oral hygiene.

Dental Implants

Dental implants are the gold standard for replacing missing teeth. A titanium post is surgically placed into your jawbone, where it fuses naturally over several months to create a permanent foundation. A custom-made crown is then attached, giving you a tooth that looks, feels, and functions exactly like a natural tooth. Implants prevent bone loss and maintain facial structure.

Root Canal Treatment

A root canal is a treatment designed to save a severely infected or decayed tooth. Contrary to popular belief, the procedure itself is virtually painless thanks to modern anesthesia and techniques. We remove the infected pulp, clean and disinfect the inner chambers, then seal the tooth to prevent further infection.

Invisalign

Invisalign uses a series of clear, removable aligners to gradually straighten your teeth without the discomfort and appearance of metal braces. Each set of aligners is worn for about two weeks before moving to the next set. The aligners are virtually invisible, so most people won't even notice you're undergoing treatment.

General dentistry encompasses all routine preventive and restorative care needed to maintain optimal oral health. Regular checkups every six months allow us to detect cavities, gum disease, and other issues early when treatment is simplest. Porcelain Veneers

Porcelain veneers are thin, custom-made shells designed to cover the front surface of teeth, instantly transforming your smile. They are an ideal solution for teeth that are stained, chipped, misaligned, worn down, uneven, or have gaps. The process typically requires two visits.
