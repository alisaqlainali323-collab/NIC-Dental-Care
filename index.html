Ask me directly →
Do you handle patient data securely? +

Yes — every form and storage system I build follows strict data-privacy practices, with encrypted forms and secure hosting from day one.

How long does a launch take? +

Most single-location practices go live in about four weeks from our first call, depending on how quickly I get content and photos from your team.

Who owns the website once it's built? +

You do. The domain and the site are yours — my monthly plans cover hosting, updates, and support, not ownership.

Can you work with our existing booking software? +

In most cases, yes. I've connected with common scheduling and practice-management tools, and I'll tell you upfront if something needs a workaround.

Do you only work with clinics in Rawalpindi and Islamabad? +

Those are my main service areas, but I work with medical practices anywhere in Pakistan — everything is remote-friendly, with calls and updates over WhatsApp or video.

What happens after launch — are we on our own? +

Not at all. Every plan includes post-launch support, and my Premium plan includes ongoing maintenance, updates, and monitoring every month.
